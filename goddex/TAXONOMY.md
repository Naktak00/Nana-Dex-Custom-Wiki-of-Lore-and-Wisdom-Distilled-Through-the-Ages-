# GodDex Taxonomy

GodDex is a comparative indexing system, not a claim that its categories are universal features of reality.

## Working Classes

### Spirit
A fragmentary or specialized expression of a larger pattern: narrow capability, local function, or partial inheritance.

### Demi-God
A sufficiently integrated archetype with durable identity across multiple stories or functions.

### Land God
A pattern whose persistence is strongly coupled to a territory, culture, institution, language, ecology, or community.

### Divine
A **personal value classification**, not an externally measurable power level.

One useful Nana-Dex reading is portability: some patterns survive the loss of their original container and continue through new hosts and media. But no one is required to share another person's Divine.

## Transmission Chain
**memory → oral tradition → manuscript → print → art → film → internet → databases → AI → ?**

At every transition:
- something may be preserved,
- something may be lost,
- something may mutate,
- something new may become possible.

**Origin ≠ Destiny. Vessel ≠ Contents.**
