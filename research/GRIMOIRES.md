# Grimoire Research Map

## Research Preference
**Knowledge density wins.**

Nana-Dex is primarily interested in:
- names,
- domains,
- attributed capabilities,
- symbols,
- hierarchy,
- relationships,
- historical transmission,
- source genealogy,
- competing interpretations.

Ritual procedure is included when it explains the architecture of a text, but operational padding is not the project's center of gravity.
