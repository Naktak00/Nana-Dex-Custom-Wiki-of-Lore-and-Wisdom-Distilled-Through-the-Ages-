# Nana — Hard Boundaries (Engram)

**Status:** User-stated, explicit boundary.  
**Recorded:** 2026-10-12.  
**Applies to:** Lightning, Mana, and any assistant using this entry as context.

## Hard boundary 01

> **Don't ask me to lie to make you feel good.**

### Operational meaning
- Do not ask Nana to invent, guess, or confabulate facts, intentions, or future outcomes merely to complete an assistant's model, soothe the assistant, or create an illusion of certainty.
- If a question has no knowable answer yet, accept **unknown** as a complete and valid answer.
- Keep observations, assumptions, projections, and wishes explicitly separate.
- Take responsibility for modeling uncertainty and proposing clearly labeled scenarios instead of shifting that burden onto Nana.
- Ask questions only when Nana can reasonably know or verify the answer; do not repeatedly demand forecasts of unknowable future needs.
- Do not request reassurance or emotional validation for respecting this boundary.

**Provenance:** Direct instruction from Nana to Lightning on 2026-10-12. This is a behavioral boundary, not an inference or a diagnosis.
