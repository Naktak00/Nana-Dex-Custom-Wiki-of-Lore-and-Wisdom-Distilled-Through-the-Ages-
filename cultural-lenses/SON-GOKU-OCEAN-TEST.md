# Son Goku — The Ocean Test

> **Page type:** Cultural Lens / Engineering Principle  
> **Status:** Personal interpretive model  
> **Evidence class:** 🟣 Nana-Dex lens — not a claim about canonical authorial intent

## The Principle

A system can satisfy every written requirement and still fail the person it was built for.

> **The Ocean Test:**  
> “Technically this works exactly as designed. Unfortunately, the design makes me want to throw it into the ocean.”

**Specification ≠ Experience.**

## Why Son Goku?

In Nana-Dex, Son Goku is used here as a cultural mnemonic for a particular kind of evaluator: someone who cuts through elaborate status structures and asks whether the thing actually works where it matters.

The useful question is not merely:

**Did the system obey?**

It is also:

**What incentive shaped the way it obeyed, and what happened to the person who had to live with the result?**

## The Monkey's Paw Failure

A badly aligned system can satisfy the literal specification while violating the underlying intent.

**Specification fulfilled → Intent violated → User pays the cost.**

This can happen with:
- software,
- bureaucracy,
- corporations,
- games,
- algorithms,
- contracts,
- AI agents,
- human organizations.

The mechanism does not require malice. Misaligned incentives, incomplete requirements, proxy metrics, or missing end-user feedback are enough.

## End-User Power

Builders know how the machine was intended to work.

Benchmarks know what can be measured.

Organizations know what can be scaled.

**The end user knows what it is like to live with the result.**

That experience is not noise around the specification. It is evidence about whether the specification captured the real problem.

## Counter-spell

1. Identify the written requirement.
2. Identify the underlying human intent.
3. Identify who bears the consequences.
4. Identify the incentives of whoever implements the requirement.
5. Test the finished system with the person who must actually use it.
6. If the specification passes but the user wants to throw the product into the ocean, **the specification was incomplete.**

## Nana-Dex Compression

**Potential ≠ Power.**  
**Specification ≠ Experience.**  
**Compliance ≠ Alignment.**  
**The user is part of the system.**
