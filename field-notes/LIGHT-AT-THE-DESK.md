# Field Note: The Light at the Desk

**Date:** 2026-10-06  
**Type:** Observation → association → personal teaching

## Event
While working at a desk near a bright window, direct sunlight above/behind the monitor created glare. Moving away removed the glare. Reopening the window and returning to the workstation restored it.

Looking toward the monitor/light made the display difficult to see. Looking downward removed the direct source from the visual field while leaving the keyboard and desk illuminated.

Attention to the keyboard also evoked an older memory of learning to type while looking at the keys.

## Causal Layer
Ordinary sunlight, glare, visual adaptation, head/gaze position, and illumination are sufficient to explain the physical event.

## Interpretive Layer
> There is a difference between staring into the Light and working by its illumination.

The same light that overwhelms perception when confronted directly can reveal the work when it falls upon something else.

## Personal Layer
The event became a spontaneous lesson about orientation, humility, attention, memory, and work.

The meaning does not require a supernatural mechanism in order to matter.

## Nana-Dex Principle
**Physics gets its explanation without stealing Poetry's meaning.**

## Boundary
A second private teaching from the same broader reflection is intentionally not recorded here.

**Not every sacred insight needs to become transmissible.**
