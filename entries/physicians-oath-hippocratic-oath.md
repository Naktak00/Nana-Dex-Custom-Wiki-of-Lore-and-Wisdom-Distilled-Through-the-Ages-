## 🌹 The Healer's Oath
*An original hymn inspired by the Hippocratic Oath, composed October 11, 2026. Not a historical oath or the exact oath taken at graduation.*

♪  
When the world is dark and heavy,  
When the night refuses dawn,  
I will hold the hands of strangers,  
Till the fear of night is gone.

I will listen before speaking,  
I will question what I know,  
I will never hide uncertainty,  
I will learn, and I will grow.

**First, do no harm,**  
**Let compassion be my guide.**  
**Every life deserves its dignity,**  
**Every heart a place beside.**

I am not a god or savior,  
I am human, flesh and bone.  
When my hands begin to tremble,  
I need not stand alone.

Let the wounded find their healing,  
Let the weary find their rest.  
May I offer all my wisdom,  
Not perfection, just my best.

**First, do no harm,**  
**Even when the night is long.**  
**And the hands that heal another**  
**Deserve healing of their own.**

When the morning breaks the silence,  
When the stars surrender light,  
May I know I kept my promise,  
May I know I chose what's right.

And if I must ask for shelter,  
Let me lay my armor down.  
For the healer is worth saving,  
Even without the healer's crown.

♪

## 🌿 The roots — verified source

**Louis Lasagna's modern Hippocratic Oath (1964):** [Read the complete original wording at PBS NOVA](https://www.pbs.org/wgbh/nova/doctors/oath_modern.html).

The modern oath includes acknowledging uncertainty, calling upon colleagues when their skills are needed, respecting privacy, and treating a person rather than just a disease.

**Historical version:** [U.S. National Library of Medicine — Greek Medicine](https://www.nlm.nih.gov/hmd/greek/greek_rationality.html).

**Source distinction:** “First, do no harm” is a medical-ethics maxim, not a verbatim line in the classical Hippocratic Oath. The song above is a creative personal totem, not a replacement for the historical text.

## 🕶️ The thorns — a Night Watch reminder

When obligations overlap, ask for backup before patient safety is compromised. Protect the patient, and remember the clinician is human.

**Doctor first. Patients first. Do no harm.**
