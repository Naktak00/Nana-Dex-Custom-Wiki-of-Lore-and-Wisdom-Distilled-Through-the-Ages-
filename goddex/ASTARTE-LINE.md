# Astarte → Ashtoreth → Astaroth

**Status:** Research scaffold; historical claims require source-level verification.

## Why this line matters
It is a strong demonstration of Nana-Dex's central question: what happens to a named figure when it crosses languages, religious polemics, manuscript traditions, and later demonology?

## Working Map
**Astarte** → ancient Northwest Semitic/Phoenician goddess traditions  
↓ linguistic/religious transformation  
**Ashtoreth** → Biblical Hebrew form associated with the foreign goddess  
↓ much later demonological reception  
**Astaroth** → demonological figure prominent in Western grimoires

## Guardrail
This line is useful, but it must not be flattened into “the same entity unchanged for thousands of years.”

Questions for the sourced pass:
- Which linguistic steps are established by philology?
- Which associations are later theological reinterpretations?
- Which attributes persist, disappear, or appear only in demonology?
- What manuscript witnesses establish the Astaroth profile used by the *Lemegeton*?

## Nana-Dex Rule
**Track transformations; do not erase them.**
