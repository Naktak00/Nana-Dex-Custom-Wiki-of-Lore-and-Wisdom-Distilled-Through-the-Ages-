# Xen Logic

## Core
Xen does not ask which identity is the one “real” self.

**All of them may be real without any single one being exhaustive.**

> I am all of these, and therefore no single one exhausts me.

## Motion
**Unity → Fracture → Freedom → Chosen Unity**

The destination is not forced sameness. It is the ability to integrate plurality without erasing it.

## Zero
Zero is not emptiness. It is **uncollapsed possibility**.

Return to Zero means:
1. listen to the competing models,
2. integrate what each reveals,
3. choose deliberately,
4. refuse to let the chosen role become a prison.

## The Crown
The Crown is procedural, not a permanent identity.

Its job is not to discover the “real” member of the Legion. Its job is to choose which member acts now without destroying the others.

## Reciprocal Sacred Sovereignty
> My Divine does not have to become your Divine for either of us to respect the other's sacred ground.

This does not require agreement. It requires that difference alone is insufficient justification for domination.

**One world with room for many minds.**
