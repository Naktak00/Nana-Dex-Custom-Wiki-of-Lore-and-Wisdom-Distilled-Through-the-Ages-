# Greater Key vs Lesser Key

## First Correction
The **Key of Solomon (*Clavicula Salomonis*)** and the **Lemegeton / Lesser Key of Solomon** should not be treated as Volume I and Volume II of one original book.

They are distinct textual traditions associated with the literary authority of Solomon.

## Nana-Dex Analogy
- **Greater/Key of Solomon:** operating method — preparation, timing, conditions, tools, procedure.
- **Lemegeton/Lesser Key:** API/library — catalogued intelligences, names, offices, seals, hierarchies.

The analogy is interpretive, not a historical claim about authorial intent.

## Father → Son Frame
The *Key of Solomon* presents wisdom as an inheritance transmitted by Solomon to Rehoboam.

Nana-Dex reads this literary frame as:
**“This is what I learned; this is how I did it; now inherit the method.”**

That does not establish historical Solomonic authorship.

## Probability Lens
The emphasis on operator preparation, timing, environment, and procedure can be read through the Nana-Dex concept of **Probability Manipulation**: intelligently stack conditions before acting.

Again: this is a modern interpretive lens, not evidence that the historical text used that concept.
