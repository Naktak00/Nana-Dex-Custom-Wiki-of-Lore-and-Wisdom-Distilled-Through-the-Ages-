# Logic Through Life

## Thesis
**Logic throughout life is the secret.**

Logic here does not mean stripping experience of poetry, faith, emotion, or sacred value. It means refusing to let any one of those domains silently impersonate another.

## Four Lenses
| Lens | Question |
|---|---|
| Evidence | What happened? |
| Interpretation | What might it mean? |
| Value | What matters to me? |
| Action | What should I do next? |

A single event can support all four lenses without requiring them to collapse into one answer.

## Knowledge
> Knowledge becomes power when experience is named, classified, preserved, and made transmissible.

And:

> Knowledge can transform the knower irreversibly.

The first statement describes transmission. The second describes consequence.

## Probability Manipulation
In Nana-Dex, **Probability Manipulation** means improving the odds of an outcome through preparation, timing, redundancy, optionality, anticipation, favorable conditions, and intelligent setup.

It does **not** mean supernatural control of probability.

## Counter-spells
A “counter-spell” is the practical answer to a recurring pattern:
**knowledge + preparation + engineering + reframing + discipline + perspective.**

The fantasy vocabulary is allowed. The mechanism must remain inspectable.
